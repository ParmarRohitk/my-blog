The much-anticipated **Fabtech Technologies IPO** allotment status has been released. Investors who participated in the public offering can now check their allotment status via multiple platforms. With a massive **oversubscription of 740.36 times**, this IPO has captured significant attention in the market.

---

## How to Check Your Allotment Status

### 1. **BSE Website**

- Visit the [BSE IPO Allotment Page](https://www.bseindia.com/investors/appli_check.aspx).
- Enter your **application number** or **PAN** to check the status.

### 2. **Registrar's Website**

- The IPO registrar is **Maashitla Securities Private Limited**.
- Visit their official website and enter the required details (such as application number or PAN) to confirm your allotment status.
