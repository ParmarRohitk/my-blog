The **National Testing Agency (NTA)** has officially released the **JEE Main 2025 City Intimation Slip** for **Session 1**, bringing relief to thousands of aspirants eagerly awaiting this crucial information. The City Intimation Slip provides details about the **city of the examination center**, enabling candidates to plan their travel and accommodations well in advance.

This step by NTA ensures that students can focus on their preparation without last-minute logistical worries.

---

## Key Highlights:

Here are the primary features of the **JEE Main 2025 City Intimation Slip**:

- **Access:** The slip is available for download on the official NTA website: [jeemain.nta.nic.in](https://jeemain.nta.nic.in).
- **City Details:** The slip specifies the **name of the city** where the candidate’s examination center is located.
- **Advance Planning:** By providing this information, candidates can arrange their **transportation** and **accommodation**, ensuring a hassle-free exam day.

---

## What to Do Next:

### 1. **Download the Slip**

Follow these steps to access your City Intimation Slip:

- Visit the official NTA website: [jeemain.nta.nic.in](https://jeemain.nta.nic.in).
- Log in using your **application number** and **password**.
- Download and save the City Intimation Slip for reference.

### 2. **Plan Your Travel**

Once you know your exam city:

- Book your **tickets** (train, bus, or flight) at the earliest to avoid last-minute price surges.
- Arrange **accommodation** near the exam center if needed.

### 3. **Familiarize Yourself with the City**

Take some time to research the city:

- Identify the **location of your examination center**.
- Plan your travel route to avoid confusion on the exam day.

### 4. **Focus on Preparation**

With logistics sorted, dedicate the remaining time to:

- Revising important topics.
- Practicing mock tests and previous year papers.
- Gaining confidence in time management during the exam.

---

## Important Notes:

- The **City Intimation Slip** is **not the Admit Card**.
- The Admit Card, which will contain details like:

  - **Exact address** of the examination center,
  - **Date and time** of the exam,
  - **Important instructions**,  
    will be released closer to the exam date.

- Candidates are advised to frequently check the official NTA website for updates.
