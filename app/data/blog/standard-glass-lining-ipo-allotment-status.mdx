## How to Check Your Allotment Status

Here’s how you can check your IPO allotment:

### 1. **BSE Website**

- Visit the [BSE Allotment Status Page](https://www.bseindia.com/investors/appli_check.aspx).
- Enter your **application number** or **PAN** to check the status.

### 2. **NSE Website**

- Visit the NSE IPO Bid Verification page.
- Follow the steps provided on the site to access your allotment status.

### 3. **Kfin Technologies**

- Go to the official website of **Kfin Technologies**, the registrar for the IPO.
- Enter your details such as **application number**, **DP ID/Client ID**, or **PAN** to check your status.
