## Ohio State Dominates Oregon in Rose Bowl Classic

**San Marino, CA** - The Ohio State Buckeyes continue to prove why they deserve a spot in the NCAA finals with a stunning **41-21** win against the Oregon Ducks during the **109th Rose Bowl Game**. Curtis warned that the Buckeyes would challenge the Ducks’ free-scoring attack, and Ohio State’s defense delivered by denying the Ducks opportunities to power their way through.

### Key Highlights

- **Ohio State's Offensive Explosion**: There was no stopping the Buckeyes’ offense on the field as the team gained significant yardage and frequently reached the end zone.
- **Strong Defensive Stand**: Ohio State’s defense neutralized the Ducks’ scoring options and created crucial turnovers on three separate occasions.
- **A Show of Strength**: The Buckeyes' win solidified their position as one of the best teams in the country, showcasing their depth, skill, and discipline on and off the field.
