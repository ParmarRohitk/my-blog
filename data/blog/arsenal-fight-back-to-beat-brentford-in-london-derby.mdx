## Arsenal Fight Back to Beat Brentford in London Derby

**London, England** - In the first match of the year that was a tough London derby across the Thames at **Gtech Community Stadium**, Arsenal came out on top with a **3-1 win** over Brentford.

The hosts began this game on the front foot and got their nose in front through the boot of **Bryan Mbeumo**. But Arsenal, as always, showed tremendous character with **Gabriel Jesus** scoring an equalizer before halftime.

The second half was dominated by the **Gunners**. This was enough for **Mikel Merino** and **Gabriel Martinelli** to complete the game and get the three points for Arsenal, which put them as the second team in the Premier League.

### Key Takeaways:

#### Arsenal's Resilience

They could have lost the match by going behind early; however, Arsenal again showed their rich form to come back and clinch a victory.

#### Jesus Continues to Shine

The Brazilian forward keeps scoring and showing he is **Arteta’s key player** for the team.

#### Brentford's Threat

Nevertheless, Brentford proved they are capable of matching the giants of the league and will pose a threat to any contender.

This success will be very helpful to **Arsenal**, especially when it comes to chasing down the **Premier League trophy**.
