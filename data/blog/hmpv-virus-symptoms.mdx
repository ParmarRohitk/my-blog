# HMPV Virus Symptoms

The following information is for educational purposes only. For medical advice or diagnosis, always consult a healthcare professional.

Human metapneumovirus (HMPV) is a respiratory virus that can cause illnesses ranging from mild cold symptoms to severe respiratory tract diseases. While not always severe, it is important to recognize symptoms, particularly for individuals at greater risk.

---

## Common Symptoms of HMPV

- **Cough:** Symptoms can range from mild to severe.
- **Runny Nose:** Nasal congestion or a runny nose is very common.
- **Fever:** Mild to moderate fever is typical, but high fever can also occur.
- **Sore Throat:** May present as soreness or irritation in the throat.
- **Difficulty Breathing:** In severe cases, especially in children, wheezing or significant breathing difficulties may occur.

---

## When to Seek Medical Attention

- **Difficulty Breathing:** If breathing becomes very difficult, seek emergency care immediately.
- **High Fever:** Persistent high fever, especially in children or infants, requires prompt medical attention.
- **Worsening Symptoms:** If symptoms worsen, reappear, or new symptoms develop, consult a healthcare provider.
- **Vulnerable Individuals:** If a person has a weakened immune system, chronic respiratory disease, or is elderly, consult a doctor immediately upon developing symptoms.

---

## Important Notes

1. **HMPV can mimic other respiratory illnesses:**  
   Symptoms are similar to common respiratory viruses, making diagnosis challenging without medical evaluation.

2. **No definitive cure:**  
   There is currently no specific treatment for HMPV. However, antiviral medications and supportive care can alleviate symptoms.

3. **Consult a healthcare professional:**  
   Always seek advice from a doctor for accurate diagnosis, especially in severe cases or for vulnerable individuals.
