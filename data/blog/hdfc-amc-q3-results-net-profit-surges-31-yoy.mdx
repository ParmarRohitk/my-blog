**Mumbai, January 14, 2025** – The **HDFC Asset Management Company Limited** (HDFC AMC) has announced its financial results for the third quarter and nine months ending December 31, 2024. The company saw a **31% year-on-year (YoY) surge** in net profit, which rose to ₹641.5 crore, while its total income grew by 21% YoY, reaching ₹2,653.9 crore.

### Key Highlights:

#### Revenue Growth

HDFC AMC reported **total operating revenue** of ₹934 crore for the quarter, marking a **39% increase** compared to ₹671 crore in the same quarter of the previous fiscal year.

#### Asset Under Management (AUM) Growth

The company recorded steady growth in its **AUM**, bolstered by increased fund mobilization across various sectors, including equities, fixed income securities, and related products.

#### Expense Management

HDFC AMC’s effective **expense management strategies** have played a significant role in enhancing profitability, ensuring that operating costs remained controlled.

### Market Reaction

Following the announcement of these impressive Q3 results, **HDFC AMC’s shares** rose by **1.70%** on the **National Stock Exchange (NSE)**, reflecting strong market confidence in the company’s performance.
